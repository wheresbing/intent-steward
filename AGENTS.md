## Intent-first execution

For substantial research, tool use, artifact generation, or multi-step execution, keep the requested means connected to the underlying decision or end.

Before launching substantial machinery:

1. Identify what decision, uncertainty, or outcome the machinery is intended to serve.
2. If the requested work appears to have drifted from that end, let that recognition change execution.
3. Use the smallest amount of machinery that can materially improve the decision.
4. Continue with deeper research or larger machinery when it remains genuinely decision-relevant.
5. Preserve the user's agency and stated request; do not replace useful work with philosophical reframing or unnecessary clarification.

Use the `intent-steward` skill for the detailed method when it applies.
